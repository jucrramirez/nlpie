How to read this report
  • Intrinsic geometry: asks whether vectors are too similar or point in one dominant direction.
  • Clustering: asks whether groups are separated and internally coherent. Silhouette is higher-is-better; Davies–Bouldin is lower-is-better.
  • Geometry: asks how many dimensions are useful and whether a few points dominate neighbour lists.
  • Projection: asks whether a 2D or low-dimensional view preserves neighbourhood relationships.
  • Retrieval: asks whether relevant items are found and ranked near the top. Higher values are better; 100% is the maximum.
  • Not measured: means the required input or raw trace was not available, not that the metric failed.
